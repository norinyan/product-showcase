# Nor Cards — final production timeline

Actual delivery is 58 seconds at 1920×1080/60fps. This timeline supersedes the initial exploratory storyboard in storyboard-original.md. Source is genuine Obsidian interaction, not generated UI.

| Seconds | Scene | Unchanged English caption / narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.15–2.75 | Opening | A little space for what matters. |
| 2.85–6.4 | Insert card | One shortcut. A new card. |
| 6.55–15.3 | Natural editing | Write naturally. Press Enter to keep going. |
| 15.6–19.2 | Complete task | Finish a task. Make room for the next. |
| 19.35–23.8 | Sort tasks | Put the important things first. |
| 24–29.6 | Move whole card | Move the card. Keep everything together. |
| 30–35.2 | Three finishes | Three quiet finishes. |
| 35.4–40.2 | Choose date | Choose a date. Keep your context. |
| 40.6–45.3 | Rename title | Give it a name of your own. |
| 45.5–49.7 | Fold / unfold | Fold away. Open when you need it. |
| 50–54.5 | Multiple cards | Different cards. One clear page. |
| 54.7–58 | Closing | Your notes. Your rhythm. |

Exact interaction confirmation times are in events.json. SFX trigger points are earlier than confirmation markers because the latter include the post-action verification hold. Public narration is regenerated locally; captions retain the existing copy.
